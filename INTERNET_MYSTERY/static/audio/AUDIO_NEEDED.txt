INTERNET MYSTERY // AUDIO

Put your three audio files in this folder:

1. ui-click.mp3
   Played when the player clicks a desktop application.

2. intro-begin.mp3
   Played when BEGIN is clicked, during the boot / analyzing sequence.

3. background.mp3
   Background music. It starts automatically when the Desktop appears.

IMPORTANT:
- Use MP3 files with exactly these names.
- If your files are WAV, either convert them to MP3 or change the filenames/paths
  in static/js/audio.js.
- No audio file is included here; add your own three files.

BACKGROUND VOLUME:
The Desktop has a small volume control in the taskbar.
The player can:
- drag the slider to lower/raise background music
- click MUTE to silence/unmute the background music

The setting is saved locally in the browser.
